Privacy Policy — Creative Intelligence Pin

Last updated: July 10, 2026

What this extension does

Creative Intelligence Pin is a Chrome extension that analyzes images you choose to pin or paste, producing a structured description of their color palette, composition, mood, typography, and visual style. Analysis is powered by Google's Gemini API.

What information we collect

DataWhen it's collectedWhere it's stored
Images you pin or paste When you click "Analyze" on a hovered image, or paste an image into the extension Sent directly to Google's Gemini API for analysis; a temporary reference URI is kept only long enough to complete the analysis
The webpage URL the image was pinned from Same as above Stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local), only used to display the source alongside the analysis
Your Gemini API key When you enter it in the extension's Options page Stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local); never transmitted to us or to any server we control
Analysis results (color, mood, composition, etc.) After Gemini completes analysis Stored locally in your browser

We do not operate a backend server that receives or stores this data. Every image and API request goes directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API — there is no Adobe-hosted or third-party intermediary.

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect or transmit your browsing history beyond the single page a given image was pinned from
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone

Third-party services

This extension sends the images you choose to analyze to Google's Gemini API. Google's use of that data is governed by Google's own privacy policy and API terms. We do not control or have visibility into Google's data retention beyond what's documented in their terms.

Permissions this extension requests, and why

PermissionWhy
Access to all websites (<all_urls>)So the extension can detect images on whatever page you're browsing — this is core to how the extension works and can't be limited to a specific list of sites
storageTo save your API key and analysis results locally in your browser
tabsTo capture a screenshot as a fallback if a hovered image can't be fetched directly (e.g. images behind a login)
scripting, activeTabTo detect images and show the "Analyze" button when you hover over one
alarmsTo keep the extension's background process alive while an analysis is running (this can take up to 90 seconds)
sidePanelTo display analysis results in Chrome's side panel

Your choices

  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time by removing the extension, or by clearing your API key from the Options page
  • You control what gets analyzed — nothing is sent to Gemini unless you explicitly click "Analyze" or paste an image

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes, and update the "Last updated" date above.
